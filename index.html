Nothing between you and the recording.

Dr. Andrew Lipinski has spent more than six decades on one question: what does a speaker add that was never on the tape? The L-50 is his answer, and it is mostly a list of things left out.

  • The LIPINSKI Square™

    A patented Class A circuit with no integrated circuits anywhere in the signal path. It is the only Class A active monitor in commercial production.

  • No DSP, no equalization

    High-voltage analogue circuitry does the work. Nothing is corrected digitally, so phase stays coherent and distortion stays low.

  • A sealed enclosure

    Bass comes from a closed box, not a port, which removes the resonance ported cabinets add.

  • Ring-radiator tweeter

    A neodymium ring radiator spreads high frequencies widely and evenly, up to 40 kHz.
