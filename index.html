Nothing between you and the recording.

Dr. Andrew Lipinski has spent more than six decades on one question: what does a speaker add that was never on the tape? The L-50 is his answer, and it is mostly a list of things left out.

  • The LIPINSKI Square™

    A patented Class A circuit with no integrated circuits anywhere in the signal path. It is the only Class A active monitor in commercial production.

  • No DSP, no equalization

    High-voltage analogue circuitry does the work. Nothing is corrected digitally, so phase stays coherent and distortion stays low.

  • A sealed enclosure

    Bass comes from a closed box, not a port, which removes the resonance ported cabinets add.

  • Ring-radiator tweeter

    A neodymium ring radiator spreads high frequencies widely and evenly, up to 40 kHz.

What is the LIPINSKI L-50, and how much does it cost?

The L-50 is a two-way active reference monitor and the only Class A active monitor in production. It covers 45 Hz to 40 kHz, uses the LIPINSKI Square amplifier with no integrated circuits in the signal path, and is offered during Art Week as a turnkey, full-frequency system for $12,800: a pair of L-50s, a Proficient Reference FRS 10-inch powered subwoofer and a WiiM Ultra streamer with cabling.

Do I need a separate amplifier for the L-50?

No. Each L-50 has its own Class A analog amplifier built in, so there is nothing to add between your source and the speakers. The turnkey system also includes the WiiM Ultra streamer and all cabling.

What is included in the $12,800 system?

A pair of L-50 powered speakers with built-in Class A amplifiers, in Obsidian Black or Arctic White; a Proficient Reference FRS 10-inch powered subwoofer; and a WiiM Ultra streamer with all cabling. Together they cover 24 Hz to 40 kHz. The Art Week price runs through December 6, 2026.

Universal Mastering uses LIPINSKI Signatures as its reference monitors, and Mobile Fidelity masters on LIPINSKI. Other users include Avatar Studios in New York, NYU Steinhardt and “The President's Own” U.S. Marine Band. Stereophile placed the L-707 in Class A of its Recommended Components.
